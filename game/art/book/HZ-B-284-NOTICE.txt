HZ-B / Issue #284 — hand-drawn control icon family

Original local vector artwork created for picross, 2026-10-20.
Editable source: tools/book_icons_hz.json.
Reproducible SVG generator: tools/z2_resources.py.
No system font, emoji, downloaded icon family or external image is used.
The source contains asymmetric curves and sparse secondary pen strokes.
The empty selection placeholder deliberately has no visible ink.

This active family supersedes the shapes of the 13 BP/SL/KB icons and adds
pagination, sorting, dropdown/scroll chevrons and checkbox/selection marks.
BP-3 composition/inputs/icons.json and tools/book_icons_kb.json remain
historical original sources; their notices and delivery evidence are retained.
