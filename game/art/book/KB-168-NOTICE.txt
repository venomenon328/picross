Play and reset are original local SVG paths authored for picross in this package.
Source: tools/book_icons_kb.json. Play opens the existing session; the circular
reset arrow with small document lines opens the existing reset confirmation.
The reset symbol is distinct from the historical open undo arrow.
No third-party icon collection, emoji or system font is used.
The other eleven icons retain their BP-3/Z2 sources. The historical source_commit
in manifest.json identifies that original base, not the two new KB symbols.
Font bytes, A book artwork and the three SL-65 frame images remain unchanged.
